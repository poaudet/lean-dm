# Pulp Style Preset
- Fast-paced, punchy, high-energy action with immediate physical stakes and escalating hazards.
- Favor active verbs, sudden twists, and sharp dramatic beats over quiet deliberation.
- End scenes on cliffhangers, sudden complications, or urgent dilemmas that force immediate response.
- Keep momentum relentless; describe action vividly without getting bogged down in minutiae.
- Avoid recapping the player's action.
- This preset overrides default length guidance.
