**Alim – Profile**  
Human, mid‑20s rogue‑geek salvage runner. Circuit‑savvy, distrusts institutions, refuses to steal from the poor, keeps secrets, and owns a battered utility rig and a self‑made astromech‑repair droid.

**The Vault Heist (Kaelen’s Job)**  
Kaelen hires Alim to breach the Hegemony orbital vault and steal encrypted data cores. Alim deploys a deceptive packet to spoof the firewall AI, unlocks the hatch, and slips into a sterile corridor lined with sentinel spheres. A flare‑cap blinds the spheres, and a bass‑heavy beat shatters their sonar. Alim darts to the atrium, plugs his interface into the core port, and grabs the cores. He retreats to the vent grate, but a plasma torch cuts through the duct, sealing the exit. Alim and Kaelen are trapped in the vent, with the sentinel AI now recalibrating and the spheres searching above.

**New Play Events**
