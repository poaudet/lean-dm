**Alim – Profile**  
Human (or near‑human) in his mid‑twenties, Alim is a rogue‑geek salvage runner who grew up dismantling decommissioned droids. He talks to machines more easily than people, overconfident with circuitry, underconfident with social cues, and keeps secrets out of habit. He refuses to steal from the poor, will break any law to protect ordinary folk, and believes every system can be outsmarted. Neutral Good, distrustful of institutions, he keeps a battered utility rig and a self‑made astromech‑repair droid he refuses to sell.

**New Play – The Vault Heist**  
Kaelen, a jittery informant with a twitching prosthetic eye, offers Alim a high‑stakes job: infiltrate the Hegemony orbital vault and steal encrypted data cores. Kaelen supplies perimeter bypass codes but needs a slicer for the internal firewall—a sentient AI that hunts intruders through the network. Alim and Kaelen have slipped in low‑profile, leaving the drifting freighter *Aurelian Spear* behind. The hatch is locked and the sentinel AI is waiting on the other side.

Alim prepares a deceptive data packet: a recursive script that floods the vault’s peripheral nodes with hundreds of tiny “corrupted” fragments, creating a chaotic system failure that draws the AI’s attention. The packet succeeds, the hatch disengages, and the corridor opens. Alim and Kaelen move quickly, but the vault’s interior is a sterile, white‑lit corridor with two hovering sentinel‑spheres that sweep the air with lethal precision. Alim uses a modified flare‑cap to blast a blinding white light across the mirror‑polished floor, saturating the atrium and temporarily disabling the spheres. The ventilation grate—smelling faintly of warm copper and old grease—remains a fallback escape route. Alim has prefetched the sector schema, mapping the ductwork for a quick retreat if things go wrong.
