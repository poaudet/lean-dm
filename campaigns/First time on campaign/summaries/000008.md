**Alim – Profile**  
Human (or near‑human), mid‑twenties, independent tech‑specialist and salvage runner on the outer rim. A rogue‑geek who grew up tearing apart decommissioned droids, Alim talks to machines more easily than people, overconfident with circuitry, underconfident with social cues, and keeps secrets out of habit. He refuses to steal from the poor, will break any law to protect ordinary folk, and believes every system can be outsmarted. Neutral Good, distrustful of institutions, he keeps a battered utility rig and a self‑made astromech‑repair droid he refuses to sell.

**New Play – The Vault Heist**  
Kaelen, a jittery informant with a twitching prosthetic eye, offers Alim a high‑stakes job: infiltrate the Hegemony orbital vault and steal encrypted data cores. Kaelen has perimeter bypass codes but needs a slicer for the internal firewall—a sentient AI that hunts intruders through the network. The vault’s outer perimeter is easy; the real challenge lies in bypassing the AI‑guarded server hub. Alim and Kaelen are approaching the vault, having left the drifting freighter *Aurelian Spear* behind. They’ve slipped in low‑profile, the hatch is locked, and the sentinel AI is waiting on the other side. Alim has prepared a deceptive data packet to distract the AI and plans to slip past it, hack the hub, retrieve the cores, and escape. The pay could buy Alim a new rig and a chassis for his droid.
