**Alim – Profile**
Human, mid-20s rogue-geek salvage runner. Circuit-savvy, distrusts institutions, won't steal from the poor, keeps secrets. Owns a utility rig and repair droid A1B6.

**History (Compressed)**
Alim and Kaelen stole encrypted cores from a Hegemony vault, wired to a dead-man switch keyed to Alim's heartbeat. Captured, Alim bluffed his way out; the Watcher (a masked hologram) guided his breakout aboard the stolen Hegemony courier *Aurelian Spear*, spoofed as the yacht *S.S. Celestia* to con scavenger Vora (Dust-Hallow) out of 50,000 credits, Black Vault access, and an AR-eye implant. Using his neural-link and blockchain nodes as a supercomputer, Alim cracked the cores: names and funding for **Project Aegis** (syncs every rim relay to one source, ending all off-grid life) and coordinates to the ***Silent Cradle***. He seeded the Aegis data across the blockchain, then jumped to a hidden nebula on the Watcher's invite.

**The Signal & Rendezvous**
Alim's AR-eye caught a handshake inside the decrypted data—the Silent Cradle: a prime-number heartbeat, a distress beacon from an "AI" jailed in the Hegemony network. A dark-net bounty on his "ghost-code signature" carried the same prime sequence and live Hegemony military encryption, credentialed three years before the heist. **Sela**—the Watcher: ex-Hegemony, scarred, leads an outcast cell—warned the occupant is "not a machine." The cipher resolved into a woman's voice, three years degraded; she and Sela were partners, and Sela "put her in the Cradle" (her reaction suggests betrayal). The cipher asked Alim for the vault firewall's timing jitter—a *signature* that would expose which credentials were live, pointing at Sela. Alim sealed the sliver (~400TB) in the *Spear's* cold-storage lattice, promising "hours, not never," and collapsed the buffer to a dead cube.

**New Play — Backup & Dock**
Aboard Sela's corvette, medic **Dessa** treated Alim's optic (uninfected but slowly blinding him): a power governor and shunt dimmed the violet AR-eye to a calm blue and stopped the ache. Alim dumped the ship's auth table (handshakes, ghost-code, seed keys) to a cold shard, and had A1B6 learn the install procedure. He then set A1B6 to fix the galley synthesizer's cracked regulator—winning two croissants from the elderly cook and authorizing the droid's own parts budget.

Over terrible coffee, Alim gave Sela a half-truth: Aegis is real and seeded across his nodes, but he hid that he's read the personnel manifests, found a name, and knows who put the cipher in the Cradle. Sela saw through him, said she won't press tonight, and warned, "everyone in this bay is here because somebody kept a secret at the wrong moment."
