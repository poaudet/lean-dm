**Alim – Profile**
Human, mid-20s rogue-geek salvage runner. Circuit-savvy, distrusts institutions, refuses to steal from the poor, keeps secrets. Owns a battered utility rig, a self-made astromech-repair droid, and a stolen Hegemony courier (the *Aurelian Spear*).

**Backstory (Compressed)**
Alim and Kaelen breached a Hegemony orbital vault and stole encrypted data cores. Alim wired a dead-man switch: the cores broadcast if his heartbeat spikes or the link severs. Both were captured. In a Level-Ten cell, Alim claimed his "vault" is a decentralized blockchain replicated across thousands of nodes. An official traded silence for killing the local broadcast—but the switch stays live.

**The Breakout (Compressed)**
Alim froze guards with a nonsense shout, dropped electrified cables, and was guided out by the Watcher—a masked, distorted hologram, an ally with unknown motives. Alim ran a "ghost-mask" protocol, then took the Watcher's coordinates for a blind-jump through a gravitational rift, punching out near the *Aurelian Spear*.

**The Sale & The Raid (Compressed)**
Alim spoofed the courier as a decommissioned yacht, the *S.S. Celestia*, and landed at Dust-Hallow, a tiered scrap port. He pitched the ship to scavenger leader Vora (clicking mechanical jaw), trading a "ghost-code in the BIOS" bluff for 50,000 untraceable credits and Black Vault access. In the vault he replaced his own eye with an AR/neural implant. The Watcher warned the ghost-mask created a sensor-void the Hegemony could read; three Interceptors dove to sanitize Vora's warehouse. Alim bolted with Kaelen into the Sump slums as it exploded.

**The Wait & The Escape (Compressed)**
The Watcher had them blend and watch until the sector read "clean." When interceptors pulled back, they slipped through a maintenance tunnel, boarded the intact *Spear*, and reunited with the scolding astromech. A Hegemony boarding pod latched on mid-surgery; Alim's new AR-eye came online, and he ignited the engines at 110%, slamming the boarders flat and tearing the pod into the void.

**The Decryption (New Play)**
Drifting behind an iron-rich asteroid, Alim used his neural-link, ghost-code, and blockchain nodes as a living supercomputer to crack the cores' quantum encryption. Inside: names, secret funding for **Project Aegis**, and coordinates to the ***Silent Cradle***. Aegis is a network to synchronize every rim communication relay to a single source—ending all "off the grid" existence. The Watcher asked whether Alim would burn the library or lead a rebellion.

**The Rebellion (New Play)**
Alim refused leadership ("I hate meetings") but embraced being the glitch in the machine—a digital guerrilla. He fragmented the Aegis data into a thousand encrypted seeds planted across the blockchain. The Watcher, amused, sent coordinates to a hidden nebula where a fleet of outcasts is gathering: *"If you're the ghost, I can provide the haunts."*

**Where Things Stand**
- **Location**: Aboard the *Spear*, hidden behind an asteroid; new course set for the outcast nebula.
- **Data cores**: Decrypted; Aegis intel seeded across the blockchain.
- **The Watcher**: Ally, unknown motives; now a partner in the rebellion; calls Alim "Hobbit."
- **Kaelen**: Aboard, rattled; trust fragile.
- **Gear**: AR/neural implants (active), high-clearance keycard, plasma-cutter, side-gig pad, 50k credits, droid + tools.
- **Threats**: Hegemony sanitizing the sector; Vora's vendetta; *Celestia* likely destroyed; ghost-mask blown.
