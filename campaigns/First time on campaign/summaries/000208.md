**Alim** — Human, mid-20s rogue-geek salvage runner. Circuit-savvy, distrusts institutions, won't steal from the poor, keeps secrets. Owns a utility rig and droid A1B6.

**Backstory**
Alim and Kaelen stole cores from a Hegemony vault (dead-man switch keyed to Alim's heartbeat); hologram **Sela** (ex-Hegemony) guided his breakout. Cores held **Project Aegis** (syncs every rim relay to one source) and coordinates to the ***Silent Cradle*** — an "AI" jailed in the Hegemony net, once Sela's partner. Alim seeded Aegis across the blockchain, fled to Sela's nebula hideout; medic **Dessa** fitted a governor/shunt to his optic (blinding him).

**Cipher, Plan & Scattering**
Alim built a biometric gate routing the cipher's carrier wave through his neural tremor — making him her translator and censor. The cipher-woman volunteered; rotations ate her memory of consent. Sela confessed the cipher *climbed in herself* — she said yes because Aegis was coming anyway. Aegis needs three things they lack: the sync schedule, the authenticating cipher, a live uplink. Auth keys for all fourteen junctions sit in a Tarsis vault. The cipher can write the schedule *wrong* (four-second drift) so Aegis fails. Deadline: nine days; Sela has three inside contacts. Alim's endgame: seed the cipher's *memory* across ten thousand dying machines — a rumour can't be killed; the cipher gets a vote.

**Orizon & Vora**
The 50k dead-drop on Orizon was bait for **Vora** (hydraulic jaw), who thinks Alim sold her a war crime. Alim sent A1B6 alone to the drop, then told her everything. Vora revealed a Hegemony "salvage assessment" team seized her inventory manifest three days after Alim fled — they're *surveying* the rim. The lower tiers have queued nine days for water; recycler four is dead — a riot is coming.

**The Deal (struck)**
Alim offered to make Vora the *face* of a rebellion centered on Orizon — a riot against the water and the Hegemony, drawing all eyes. Vora agreed on her terms: her name flows as *hers* (Vora Kesh of Dust-Hallow, not "Alim's fence"); her Berwick hauler (fails inspection, walks a crew onto Tarsis) comes with pilot **Osei** and clean paperwork. She'll strip the registry, pull the transponder, and plant a coolant fault (number-three loop) fixable in four minutes. Alim goes in as **Dell Rask**, a contractor, dermal tint over his Hegemony optic (holds ~4 days).
