**Alim – Profile**
Human, mid-20s rogue-geek salvage runner. Circuit-savvy, distrusts institutions, refuses to steal from the poor, keeps secrets. Owns a battered utility rig, a self-made astromech-repair droid, and a stolen Hegemony courier.

**Backstory (Compressed)**
Alim and Kaelen breached a Hegemony orbital vault and stole encrypted data cores. Alim wired a dead-man switch: the cores broadcast if his heartbeat spikes or the link severs. Both were captured. In a Level-Ten cell, Alim claimed his "vault" is a decentralized blockchain replicated across thousands of nodes. An official traded silence for killing the local broadcast—but the switch stays live. A pulsing corridor camera hinted at a hidden watcher.

**The Breakout & Escape (Compressed)**
Alim lured the watcher with a fake technical yarn, froze guards by shouting "We're not burglars—we are hobbitses!", and dropped electrified cables from a ceiling panel. A masked, distorted hologram—the Watcher—guided them out, an ally with unknown motives. Alim grabbed the guards' black-market side-gig pad, ran a "ghost-mask" protocol to blur his signature into drone noise, and shoved Kaelen down a disposal chute as a distraction. He bypassed biometrics, punched through the atmospheric shield, and escaped. The Watcher warned the Hegemony would lock every jump-gate and gave coordinates for a blind-jump through a gravitational rift. Alim overclocked the drive and jumped—a violent transit that knocked out the hologram. They punched out near the battered freighter *Aurelian Spear*.

**The Sale (New Play)**
Before docking at Orizon-4, Alim spoofed the courier's transponder as a decommissioned Hegemony luxury yacht—the *S.S. Celestia*—to hide from pursuit and inflate its value. He landed at the lower docks of **Dust-Hallow**, a tiered scrap-metal port city. Playing an aristocratic collector, he pitched the ship's "unique modifications" as a rare engineering marvel. The scavenger leader—**Vora**, a burly woman with a clicking mechanical jaw—led him into her cavernous warehouse.

Alim escalated: he floated the *Aurelian Spear* as a second salvage and asked for parts, intel, and a data stash instead of just credits. Vora demanded proof. Alim gave half-truths—the *Spear* is his home/workshop, the courier's mods live in "ghost-code woven into the BIOS," invisible to Hegemony scans. Vora bought it: "A ghost-ship and a fortress-freighter. I'm in." Deal struck—50,000 untraceable credits, 48-hour access to her military-grade "Black Vault," and a threat to sell his organs if the BIOS claim is false.

In the Black Vault, Alim found neural interfaces, thruster coils, and high-end ocular implants. He's set on splicing an AR-overlay with a neural-link processor, bridged by courier ghost-code—an embedded AI + AR interface to speed up planning now that they're Priority One fugitives. Kaelen is horrified; Alim's going through with it.

**Where Things Stand**
- **Location**: Dust-Hallow, Orizon-4, inside Vora's Black Vault. Mid-procurement of AR/neural augments.
- **Data cores**: Secured in the cloud via dead-man switch; still live.
- **The Watcher**: Message survived the jump: "Don't get too comfortable on Orizon. I'm coming for the cores. Keep your eyes open, Hobbit." Motives unknown; ally status uncertain.
- **Kaelen**: Aboard, rattled, wants a hospital and a drink; trust still fragile.
- **Gear**: High-clearance keycard, plasma-cutter, black-market side-gig pad, disguised courier (*S.S. Celestia*), 50k credits, Black Vault access.
- **Threats**: Hegemony jump-gate lockdown; Sentinel AI may recalibrate; ghost-mask blur is fragile and time-limited; *Celestia* disguise could unravel; Vora's organ-sale threat if the BIOS bluff fails.
