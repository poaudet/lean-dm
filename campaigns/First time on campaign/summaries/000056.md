**Alim – Profile**
Human, mid‑20s rogue‑geek salvage runner. Circuit‑savvy, distrusts institutions, refuses to steal from the poor, keeps secrets, owns a battered utility rig and a self‑made astromech‑repair droid.

**Earlier Heist (Compressed)**
Alim and Kaelen breached the Hegemony orbital vault, spoofed the firewall AI, and stole encrypted data cores. Alim triggered a dead‑man switch: the cores broadcast if his heartbeat spikes or the connection severs. Both were captured. In a Level‑Ten cell, Alim revealed his "vault" is a decentralized blockchain replicated across thousands of nodes. A high‑ranking official negotiated silence for killing the local broadcast—but the switch stays live. A pulsing corridor camera hinted at a hidden watcher.

**The Breakout**
Alim fed Kaelen a fake technical yarn (parity bits, a "quantum key," a salt‑hash seed) to lure the watcher, who'd been monitoring his blockchain upload. Blast doors sealed Alim and two guards in an airlock kill‑box. Alim shouted "We're not burglars—we are hobbitses!" to freeze them; a ceiling panel dropped electrified cables, knocking both guards out. A masked, distorted hologram—**the Watcher**—appeared and guided them into a service hatch, revealing itself as an active ally with unknown motives.

**New Play**
Alim doubled back to snatch the guards' **side‑gig pad**: a black‑market terminal with signal‑booster and encrypted relay, a key to the station's smuggling underbelly. The Watcher led them to a maintenance junction above the hangar bay, which was swarming with interceptors. Alim took time for visual recon, mapping patrol‑drone cycles and refueling‑arm timing, and ran a "ghost‑mask" protocol to blur his signature into generic maintenance‑drone noise—buying a few seconds of invisibility at launch. He picked a sleek, unmarked fast‑attack courier ship (minimal crew, high thrust, blends into the debris field). For revenge—Kaelen had thrown him at a guard earlier—Alim shoved Kaelen down a disposal chute onto the courier's cargo ramp. Kaelen's screaming panic became a perfect distraction, drawing the crew and a patrol drone. Alim slid down after him; the coast is clear for about five seconds.

**Where Things Stand**
- **Data cores**: Secured in the cloud via dead‑man switch; still live.
- **The Watcher**: Masked hologram, active ally guiding them; motives unknown.
- **Kaelen**: Free, rattled, just used as a distraction; trust still fragile; motives unclear.
- **Escape**: At the courier ship in the hangar bay; window of clear coast ~5 seconds and closing.
- **Sentinel AI**: May recalibrate and pursue.
- **Security**: Dampening field blocks wireless; two guards down; hangar swarming with interceptors; reinforcements likely.
- **Gear**: High‑clearance keycard, plasma‑cutter, and the black‑market side‑gig pad.
- **Alim's signature**: Blurred via ghost‑mask protocol; disguise is fragile and time‑limited.
