**Alim – Profile**
Human, mid-20s rogue-geek salvage runner. Circuit-savvy, distrusts institutions, refuses to steal from the poor, keeps secrets. Owns a battered utility rig, a self-made astromech-repair droid, and a stolen Hegemony courier.

**Backstory (Compressed)**
Alim and Kaelen breached a Hegemony orbital vault and stole encrypted data cores. Alim wired a dead-man switch: the cores broadcast if his heartbeat spikes or the link severs. Both were captured. In a Level-Ten cell, Alim claimed his "vault" is a decentralized blockchain replicated across thousands of nodes. An official traded silence for killing the local broadcast—but the switch stays live. A pulsing corridor camera hinted at a hidden watcher.

**The Breakout & Escape (Compressed)**
Alim lured the watcher with a fake technical yarn, froze guards by shouting "We're not burglars—we are hobbitses!", and dropped electrified cables from a ceiling panel. A masked, distorted hologram—the Watcher—guided them out, an ally with unknown motives. Alim grabbed the guards' black-market side-gig pad (signal-booster, encrypted relay), ran a "ghost-mask" protocol to blur his signature into drone noise, and shoved Kaelen down a disposal chute as a distraction. He bypassed biometrics with the pad, punched through the atmospheric shield, and escaped. The Watcher warned the Hegemony would lock every jump-gate and gave coordinates for a blind-jump through a gravitational rift. Alim overclocked the drive and jumped—a violent transit that knocked out the hologram. They punched out near the battered freighter *Aurelian Spear*.

**New Play – The Sale**
Before docking at Orizon-4, Alim spoofed the courier's transponder, disguising it as a decommissioned Hegemony luxury yacht—the *S.S. Celestia*—to hide from pursuit and inflate its value. He landed at the lower docks of **Dust-Hallow**, a tiered scrap-metal port city. Scavengers swarmed the "luxury yacht." Alim took the lead in negotiations, playing an aristocratic collector, and hinted the ship has "unique modifications" that would've been a massive headache to acquire—reframing it as a rare engineering marvel. The scavenger leader—a burly woman with a clicking mechanical jaw—is intrigued and led him into her cavernous warehouse, filled with ship skeletons and a flickering holographic pricing board. Kaelen, shoved back aboard, has "a very bad feeling" about being robbed.

**Where Things Stand**
- **Location**: Dust-Hallow, Orizon-4, inside the scavenger leader's warehouse/pricing room. Mid-negotiation to sell the *Celestia*.
- **Data cores**: Secured in the cloud via dead-man switch; still live.
- **The Watcher**: Sent a message that survived the jump: "Don't get too comfortable on Orizon. I'm coming for the cores. Keep your eyes open, Hobbit." Motives unknown; ally status uncertain.
- **Kaelen**: Aboard, rattled, wants a hospital and a drink; trust still fragile.
- **Gear**: High-clearance keycard, plasma-cutter, black-market side-gig pad, and the disguised courier (*S.S. Celestia*).
- **Threats**: Hegemony jump-gate lockdown; Sentinel AI may recalibrate; ghost-mask signature blur is fragile and time-limited; the *Celestia* disguise could unravel under scrutiny.
