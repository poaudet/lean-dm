**Alim – Profile**
Human, mid‑20s rogue‑geek salvage runner. Circuit‑savvy, distrusts institutions, refuses to steal from the poor, keeps secrets, owns a battered utility rig and a self‑made astromech‑repair droid.

**Earlier Heist (Compressed)**
Alim and Kaelen breached the Hegemony orbital vault, spoofed the firewall AI, and stole encrypted data cores. A flare‑cap blinded sentinel spheres; a bass‑heavy beat shattered their sonar. Alim triggered a dead‑man switch: the cores broadcast if his heartbeat spikes or the connection severs. An armored security droid captured them both. In a Level‑Ten holding cell, Alim revealed his "vault" is a decentralized blockchain—cores replicated across thousands of nodes, the switch a global broadcast. A high‑ranking Hegemony official, unable to silence it locally, negotiated: kill the broadcast for silence, but the switch stays live. A pulsing corridor camera hinted at a hidden watcher.

**New Play**
Alim fed Kaelen a fake technical yarn—parity bits, fragmented shards, a "quantum key," a salt‑hash seed that would recursively encrypt the data into a void within the hour unless intercepted. The guards dismissed it as panicked rambling, but the camera's pulse quickened: the watcher was listening, lured by the promise that only Alim could save the prize. Corridor lights flickered; blast doors slammed shut, sealing Alim and two guards in a security airlock—a kill‑box. To buy time, Alim shouted "We're not burglars—we are hobbitses!" The absurdity froze the guards for three seconds. A ceiling panel erupted; electrified cables dropped and shocked both guards unconscious. A masked, distorted holographic figure appeared: "The 'hobbitses' act was a bit much, but the timing was perfect. Move." Alim snatched a high‑clearance keycard and a handheld plasma‑cutter from the fallen guards, grabbed Kaelen, and dove into an undocumented service hatch. Sirens wailed as the watcher guided them through the station's veins toward a secondary launch bay, revealing they'd been monitoring Alim's blockchain upload all along.
