**Alim – Profile**
Human, mid-20s rogue-geek salvage runner. Circuit-savvy, distrusts institutions, won't steal from the poor, keeps secrets. Owns a battered utility rig, a repair droid, and a stolen Hegemony courier (the *Aurelian Spear*).

**Backstory (Compressed)**
Alim and Kaelen breached a Hegemony vault and stole encrypted data cores wired to a dead-man switch (fires if his heartbeat spikes or the link severs). Captured, Alim spun a "blockchain across thousands of nodes" bluff; an official traded silence for killing the local broadcast—but the switch stays live.

**Breakout, Sale & Escape (Compressed)**
Guided out by the Watcher (then a masked hologram), Alim ran a "ghost-mask" protocol and blind-jumped to the *Spear*. Spoofing it as a yacht (*S.S. Celestia*), he sold scavenger leader Vora (Dust-Hallow) a "ghost-code" bluff for 50,000 credits, Black Vault access, and an AR-eye implant. The ghost-mask read as a sensor-void; Interceptors torched Vora's warehouse. Fleeing, Alim tore off a boarding pod at 110% burn and—behind an iron-rich asteroid—used his neural-link, ghost-code, and blockchain nodes as a living supercomputer to crack the cores: names, funding for **Project Aegis**, and coordinates to the ***Silent Cradle***. Aegis syncs every rim relay to one source, ending all "off-grid" existence. Alim split the Aegis data into a thousand encrypted seeds across the blockchain, then set course for a hidden nebula on the Watcher's invite.

**The Signal**
En route, Alim's AR-eye caught a handshake from *inside* the decrypted data—from the Silent Cradle: a prime-number heartbeat, a distress beacon from an "AI" imprisoned in the Hegemony network. He quarantined it in an encrypted "voicemail" buffer, unanswered. A dark-net bounty on a "ghost-code signature" (last seen leaving Orizon) carried the *same prime sequence* and live-issued Hegemony military encryption—credentialed three years ago, before the heist existed.

**New Play — The Rendezvous**
At the nebula, five mismatched ships wait. A suspicious woman hailed; Alim identified as "the hobbitses." She's **Sela**—the Watcher all along: ex-Hegemony, scarred, commands this outcast cell. She warned the buffer's occupant is "not a machine." Alim opened a narrow read-only window (no execution path; firewall layers held in stasis). The cipher resolved into a woman's voice—fractured, three years degraded. She and Sela were partners; Sela "put her in the Cradle" three years ago (installed, left, or betrayed—unresolved, and Sela's reaction suggests betrayal). The cipher greeted Sela ("You got old"), then turned to Alim: she needs the **vault firewall's timing jitter**—not the key—before Sela decides she's a weapon.

**Where Things Stand**
- **Location**: *Spear* holding at the nebula lane; docking corridor extended, unpowered.
- **The ask**: Jitter is a *signature*—handing it over helps the cipher but exposes which credentials were live in the vault, pointing at Sela.
- **Sela**: Hasn't drawn her sidearm; ordered Alim to seal the buffer. Watching whether he obeys her or the thing in the box.
- **Kaelen**: Watching two armed parties negotiate over a ghost; unaware of the AI's true nature or full Aegis scope.
- **Data cores**: Decrypted; Aegis intel seeded across the blockchain.
- **Gear**: AR/neural implants (active), high-clearance keycard, plasma-cutter, side-gig pad, 50k credits, droid + tools.
- **Threats**: Hegemony sanitizing the sector; Vora's vendetta (alive); *Celestia* likely destroyed; ghost-mask blown.
