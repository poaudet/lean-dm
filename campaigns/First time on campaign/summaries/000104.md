**Alim – Profile**
Human, mid-20s rogue-geek salvage runner. Circuit-savvy, distrusts institutions, refuses to steal from the poor, keeps secrets. Owns a battered utility rig, a self-made astromech-repair droid, and a stolen Hegemony courier.

**Backstory (Compressed)**
Alim and Kaelen breached a Hegemony orbital vault and stole encrypted data cores. Alim wired a dead-man switch: the cores broadcast if his heartbeat spikes or the link severs. Both were captured. In a Level-Ten cell, Alim claimed his "vault" is a decentralized blockchain replicated across thousands of nodes. An official traded silence for killing the local broadcast—but the switch stays live. A pulsing corridor camera hinted at a hidden watcher.

**The Breakout (Compressed)**
Alim lured the watcher with a fake technical yarn, froze guards by shouting "We're not burglars—we are hobbitses!", and dropped electrified cables from a ceiling panel. A masked, distorted hologram—the Watcher—guided them out, an ally with unknown motives. Alim ran a "ghost-mask" protocol to blur his signature into drone noise, then took the Watcher's coordinates for a blind-jump through a gravitational rift. The violent transit knocked out the hologram; they punched out near the freighter *Aurelian Spear*.

**The Sale (Compressed)**
Before docking at Orizon-4, Alim spoofed the courier's transponder as a decommissioned Hegemony yacht—the *S.S. Celestia*. He landed at the lower docks of **Dust-Hallow**, a tiered scrap-metal port, and pitched the ship to scavenger leader **Vora** (burly, clicking mechanical jaw). He floated the *Spear* as second salvage, asking for parts, intel, and a data stash. Vora bought the "ghost-code in the BIOS" bluff: 50,000 untraceable credits, 48-hour access to her "Black Vault," and a threat to sell his organs if the claim is false.

**The Implants & The Raid**
In the Black Vault, Alim chose to replace his own eye—an AR-overlay with a neural-link processor, bridged by courier ghost-code, for an embedded AI + HUD. Kaelen is horrified. The Watcher warned the ghost-mask didn't hide the ship—it created a sensor-void, a "hole" a High-Inquisitor's array reads as a neon sign. Three Hegemony Interceptors dove for Vora's warehouse to sanitize, not arrest. Alim flashed Vora a naive grin ("My bad!") and bolted with Kaelen into the "Sump," the lower slums, as the warehouse exploded.

**The Wait & The Return**
The Watcher ordered them to blend and watch: the Hegemony hunts something left in the vault, moving once the sector reads "clean." Alim noted troops cluster on energy signatures—hunting tech, not people. When interceptors pulled back, the Watcher pointed to a derelict maintenance tunnel. They slipped through the Sump, reached the shielded berth, boarded the intact *Spear*, reunited with the scolding astromech, and sealed the hatch.

**The Surgery & The Escape (New Play)**
Alim ran a full diagnostic on his droid, then lay on the medical slab. No anesthetic—just a searing flash, a digital *click*, and his new AR-eye came online: tactical overlays, ship vitals, and a HUD synced to his blinks. As he marveled, the eye flashed red—a Hegemony "Surgical Strike" boarding pod had latched onto the hull, torching through the secondary airlock. The Watcher warned they'd tagged the ship before the vault and brought a disruptor to sever the blockchain. Alim answered by singing *"I believe I can flyyyyy!"*—masking a neural command to the thrusters. He ignited the engines at 110%, slamming the boarders flat and ripping the pod off into the void.

**Where Things Stand**
- **Location**: Aboard the *Aurelian Spear*, burning hard away from Dust-Hallow. Pursuit likely.
- **Data cores**: Secured in the cloud via dead-man switch; still live.
- **The Watcher**: Ally, unknown motives; "coming for the cores," calls Alim "Hobbit."
- **Kaelen**: Aboard, rattled; trust fragile.
- **Gear**: AR/neural implants (installed, active), high-clearance keycard, plasma-cutter, side-gig pad, 50k credits, droid + tools.
- **Threats**: Hegemony sanitizing the sector; Vora's vendetta; *Celestia* likely destroyed; ghost-mask blown.
