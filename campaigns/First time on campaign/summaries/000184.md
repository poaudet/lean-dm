**Alim** — Human, mid-20s rogue-geek salvage runner. Circuit-savvy, distrusts institutions, won't steal from the poor, keeps secrets. Owns a utility rig and repair droid A1B6.

**Backstory**
Alim and Kaelen stole encrypted cores from a Hegemony vault (dead-man switch keyed to Alim's heartbeat). Masked hologram **Sela** (ex-Hegemony) guided his breakout aboard the courier *Aurelian Spear*. Cores held **Project Aegis** (syncs every rim relay to one source, ending off-grid life) and coordinates to the ***Silent Cradle*** — a woman's voice, three years degraded, an "AI" jailed in the Hegemony net, once Sela's partner. Alim sealed the ~400TB sliver in cold storage, seeded Aegis across the blockchain, and fled to Sela's nebula hideout. Medic **Dessa** fitted a governor/shunt to his optic (slowly blinding him), left a spare shunt. Alim copied the ship's auth table to a cold shard; Sela saw through his half-truth.

**The Translation Lattice**
Alim built a biometric gate routing the cipher's carrier wave through his neural tremor — static to Sela's array, whole to Alim — making him her only translator and censor. Kaelen's warning: "you decide what a trapped woman is allowed to say." Dessa: not blind yet.

**The Revelations**
Sela confessed the cipher-woman *volunteered* as the unextractable cipher. The woman answers "Neither" — three years of rotations ate the memory of saying yes. She had to ask the date (Fourteen Kaleth, 4100 hrs). Her warning: Alim chooses between two people who each hold half a memory — and one has been awake long enough to write the other.

**The Pact**
Alim made cooperation the price of trust. Sela offered a signal-shielded cold room (deck four) where the cipher runs on hardware only Alim can reach. The cipher's price: *witnesses* — she listens to every Aegis conversation. Agreed.

**The Plan (Tarsis)**
Aegis needs three things they lack: the relay sync schedule, the authenticating cipher, a live uplink. Auth keys for all fourteen junctions sit in a vault on **Tarsis Station**. The cipher generates the schedule and can write it *wrong* (four-second drift) so Aegis listens to nothing — until an audit finds the error in her. Deadline: nine days.

**Infiltration**
Sela has three inside contacts (a Tarsis supply clerk, a relay tech, one unnamed). Don't build a riot — ride one already coming (ration queues, dock fees, a water-recycler failure). Be *boring*: buy the worst ship in the yard so Hegemony paperwork walks them in. Alim's 50k credits sit in a dead-drop on a world where **Vora** (hydraulic jaw) wants him dead.

**Plan B — Scattering**
Alim proposes flooding dead relays with tiny isolated "pocket networks" (handshakes, not data) so Aegis covers a map that stopped matching reality. But releasing the cipher into it means she wakes as ten thousand fragments — "a rumour instead of a person." Choice: *die free* (scattering) or *live in a box* (cold room, gamble on four seconds).

**Where Things Stand**
- **Location**: *Spear* docked at Sela's corvette.
- **Gear**: AR/neural implants (governor + spare shunt), high-clearance keycard, plasma-cutter, side-gig pad, 50k credits (dead-drop), droid A1B6, cold-shard auth backup, biometric translation lattice.
- **Threats**: Hegemony sanitizing the sector; Vora's vendetta; *Celestia* destroyed; ghost-mask blown; vault job a probable trap.
- **Next**: Alim answers Sela — which plan does he recommend?
