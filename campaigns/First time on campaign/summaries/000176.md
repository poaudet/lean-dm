**Alim – Profile**
Human, mid-20s rogue-geek salvage runner. Circuit-savvy, distrusts institutions, won't steal from the poor, keeps secrets. Owns a utility rig and repair droid A1B6.

**History**
Alim and Kaelen stole encrypted cores from a Hegemony vault (dead-man switch keyed to Alim's heartbeat). The Watcher—masked hologram **Sela**, ex-Hegemony—guided his breakout aboard the courier *Aurelian Spear*. The cores held **Project Aegis** (syncs every rim relay to one source, ending off-grid life) and coordinates to the ***Silent Cradle***. Alim seeded Aegis across the blockchain and fled to Sela's nebula hideout.

**The Cradle**
A woman's voice, three years degraded—an "AI" jailed in the Hegemony net, once Sela's partner. Alim sealed the ~400TB sliver in the *Spear's* cold storage.

**Backup & Dock**
Aboard Sela's corvette, medic **Dessa** fitted a governor/shunt to Alim's optic (slowly blinding him). Alim copied the ship's auth table to a cold shard, gave Sela a half-truth—Aegis is real and seeded—hiding that he knows who jailed the cipher. She saw through him.

**The Choice**
If Alim does nothing, the woman goes dark at the next key rotation and Sela loses her only leverage (her cell runs on ending Aegis). **Deadline: choose before Sela reopens the buffer.**

**Kaelen**
Trust provisional—earned by competence, held back because he threw Alim to the guards and has an unknown past. His revelation: the vault intel came unsolicited to his dead-drop 11 weeks pre-job, no broker cut. He assumed a Hegemony test, but "they don't send a courier for that—they send a cleanup team."

**New Play – The Translation Lattice**
Alim built a biometric gate routing the cipher's carrier wave through his neural tremor: to Sela's array she's static, to Alim she's whole—making him her only translator and censor. Kaelen's warning: "you decide what a trapped woman is allowed to say—three days ago that was the Hegemony's job." Dessa's optic check: not blind yet; she left a spare shunt ("they always fail").

**The Revelations**
Sela confessed the cipher-woman *volunteered*—her Aegis-team partner, put forward as the unextractable cipher—and can't say if that makes her a traitor or the only one who trusted her. The cipher-woman answers "Neither": she won't grant that kindness, because she doesn't remember saying yes—three years of rotations ate the moment. She had to ask Sela the date (Fourteen Kaleth, 4100 hrs), confirmed it, yet "if I'd been there, I'd have had it." Her warning: Alim chooses between two people who each hold half a memory—and one has been awake long enough to write the other.

**Where Things Stand**
- **Location**: *Spear* docked at Sela's corvette.
- **Sela**: Awaiting talks on Aegis, the Cradle, the cipher's fate.
- **Kaelen**: Confessed the setup; unaware of the AI's nature or full Aegis scope.
- **Gear**: AR/neural implants (governor + spare shunt), high-clearance keycard, plasma-cutter, side-gig pad, 50k credits, droid A1B6, cold-shard auth backup, biometric translation lattice.
- **Threats**: Hegemony sanitizing the sector; Vora's vendetta; *Celestia* destroyed; ghost-mask blown; vault job a probable trap.
- **Next**: Alim's turn to question both Sela and the cipher-woman.
