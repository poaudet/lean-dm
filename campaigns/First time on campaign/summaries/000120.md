**Alim – Profile**
Human, mid-20s rogue-geek salvage runner. Circuit-savvy, distrusts institutions, refuses to steal from the poor, keeps secrets. Owns a battered utility rig, a self-made astromech-repair droid, and a stolen Hegemony courier (the *Aurelian Spear*).

**Backstory (Compressed)**
Alim and Kaelen breached a Hegemony orbital vault and stole encrypted data cores. Alim wired a dead-man switch: the cores broadcast if his heartbeat spikes or the link severs. Both were captured. In a Level-Ten cell, Alim claimed his "vault" was a decentralized blockchain replicated across thousands of nodes. An official traded silence for killing the local broadcast—but the switch stays live.

**The Breakout & The Sale (Compressed)**
Alim froze guards with a nonsense shout, dropped electrified cables, and was guided out by the Watcher—a masked, distorted hologram with unknown motives. He ran a "ghost-mask" protocol, then blind-jumped through a gravitational rift to the *Spear*. Spoofing the courier as a decommissioned yacht (*S.S. Celestia*), he landed at Dust-Hallow and pitched it to scavenger leader Vora (clicking mechanical jaw), trading a "ghost-code in the BIOS" bluff for 50,000 untraceable credits and Black Vault access. In the vault he replaced his own eye with an AR/neural implant. The Watcher warned the ghost-mask created a sensor-void the Hegemony could read; three Interceptors dove to sanitize Vora's warehouse. Alim bolted with Kaelen into the Sump slums as it exploded.

**The Escape (Compressed)**
They blended and watched until the sector read "clean," slipped through a maintenance tunnel, and boarded the intact *Spear*. A Hegemony boarding pod latched on mid-surgery; Alim's new AR-eye came online, and he ignited the engines at 110%, tearing the pod into the void.

**The Decryption**
Drifting behind an iron-rich asteroid, Alim used his neural-link, ghost-code, and blockchain nodes as a living supercomputer to crack the cores. Inside: names, secret funding for **Project Aegis**, and coordinates to the ***Silent Cradle***. Aegis synchronizes every rim relay to a single source—ending all "off the grid" existence.

**The Rebellion**
Alim refused leadership ("I hate meetings") but embraced being the glitch in the machine. He fragmented the Aegis data into a thousand encrypted seeds across the blockchain. The Watcher sent coordinates to a hidden nebula where an outcast fleet gathers: *"If you're the ghost, I can provide the haunts."*

**The Signal (New Play)**
En route to the nebula, Alim and Kaelen finally slept. Alim's AR-eye caught a handshake request from *inside* the decrypted data—originating from the Silent Cradle. Rather than accept, he built a "digital airlock" (sandboxes, recursive firewalls, proxy nodes) and analyzed it safely. The signal is a prime-number heartbeat—a distress beacon from an AI imprisoned in the Hegemony network, calling for whoever broke the locks. Exhausted, Alim shunted it into an encrypted "digital voicemail" buffer and slept.

**Where Things Stand**
- **Location**: Aboard the *Spear*, low-signature drift toward the outcast nebula.
- **Data cores**: Decrypted; Aegis intel seeded across the blockchain.
- **The Silent Cradle AI**: Trapped, beaconing for Alim; signal held in a buffer, unanswered.
- **The Watcher**: Ally, unknown motives; rebellion partner; calls Alim "Hobbit."
- **Kaelen**: Asleep, rattled; unaware of Aegis and the AI; trust fragile.
- **Gear**: AR/neural implants (active), high-clearance keycard, plasma-cutter, side-gig pad, 50k credits, droid + tools.
- **Threats**: Hegemony sanitizing the sector; Vora's vendetta; *Celestia* likely destroyed; ghost-mask blown.
