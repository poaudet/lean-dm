**Alim – Profile**
Human, mid-20s rogue-geek salvage runner. Circuit-savvy, distrusts institutions, won't steal from the poor, keeps secrets. Owns a utility rig and a repair droid.

**History (Compressed)**
Alim and Kaelen stole encrypted cores from a Hegemony vault, wired to a dead-man switch keyed to Alim's heartbeat (fires if it spikes or the link severs). Captured, Alim bluffed a "blockchain across thousands of nodes." The Watcher (a masked hologram) guided his breakout to the stolen Hegemony courier *Aurelian Spear*, spoofed as a yacht (*S.S. Celestia*) to con scavenger Vora (Dust-Hallow) out of 50,000 credits, Black Vault access, and an AR-eye implant. Behind an asteroid, Alim used his neural-link and blockchain nodes as a supercomputer to crack the cores: names, funding for **Project Aegis**, and coordinates to the ***Silent Cradle***. Aegis syncs every rim relay to one source, ending all off-grid life. Alim seeded the Aegis data across the blockchain, then jumped to a hidden nebula on the Watcher's invite.

**The Signal & Rendezvous**
En route, Alim's AR-eye caught a handshake from inside the decrypted data—the Silent Cradle: a prime-number heartbeat, a distress beacon from an "AI" jailed in the Hegemony network. He quarantined it in an encrypted buffer. A dark-net bounty on his "ghost-code signature" carried the same prime sequence and live Hegemony military encryption—credentialed three years before the heist.

**Sela**—the Watcher all along: ex-Hegemony, scarred, leads an outcast cell—warned the occupant is "not a machine." Alim opened a read-only window; the cipher resolved into a woman's voice, three years degraded. She and Sela were partners; Sela "put her in the Cradle" three years ago (her reaction suggests betrayal). The cipher greeted Sela ("You got old"), then asked Alim for the vault firewall's timing jitter—not the key—before Sela decides she's a weapon.

**New Play — Containment & Dock**
Alim analyzed containment: the cipher is a sliver (~400TB in a timing loop), small enough for the *Spear's* cold-storage lattice—but a clock lets it reach through his replicated seeds; stasis teaches nothing. He told her he's sealing her to learn what she is, promising "hours, not never." She replied, "You're the first to say hours instead of never." He collapsed the buffer to a dead cube. Sela, hand off her sidearm, invited the *Spear* to a warm dock.

Aboard the corvette, medic **Dessa** found the optic uninfected but slowly blinding Alim. She offered a power governor and shunt to save the eye, plus a signal-shaping module filtering inbound carrier waves—but requires re-authorizing every ship system by hand.

**Where Things Stand**
- **Location**: *Spear* docked at Sela's corvette, nebula lane.
- **The ask**: The jitter is a *signature*—giving it helps the cipher but exposes which credentials were live, pointing at Sela.
- **Sela**: Waiting to talk Aegis, the Cradle, the cipher's fate.
- **Kaelen**: Aboard; unaware of the AI's nature or full Aegis scope.
- **Gear**: AR/neural implants (active), high-clearance keycard, plasma-cutter, side-gig pad, 50k credits, droid + tools.
- **Threats**: Hegemony sanitizing the sector; Vora's vendetta (alive); *Celestia* likely destroyed; ghost-mask blown.
