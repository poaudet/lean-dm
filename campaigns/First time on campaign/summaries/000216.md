**Alim** — Human, mid-20s rogue-geek salvage runner. Circuit-savvy, distrusts institutions, won't steal from the poor, keeps secrets. Owns a utility rig and droid A1B6.

**Backstory**
Alim and Kaelen stole cores from a Hegemony vault (dead-man switch keyed to Alim's heartbeat); hologram **Sela** (ex-Hegemony) guided his breakout. Cores held **Project Aegis** (syncs every rim relay to one source) and coordinates to the ***Silent Cradle***. Alim seeded Aegis across the blockchain, fled to Sela's nebula hideout; medic **Dessa** fitted a governor/shunt to his optic (blinding him).

**Cipher & Plan**
A biometric gate routes the cipher's carrier wave through Alim's neural tremor — making him her translator and censor. The cipher-woman climbed in herself; rotations ate her memory of consent. Aegis needs three things they lack: the sync schedule, the authenticating cipher, a live uplink. Auth keys for all fourteen junctions sit in a Tarsis vault. The cipher can write the schedule *wrong* (four-second drift) so Aegis fails. Deadline: ~8 days; Sela has three inside contacts. Endgame: seed the cipher's *memory* across ten thousand dying machines — a rumour can't be killed.

**Vora & the Deal**
The 50k Orizon dead-drop was bait for **Vora** (hydraulic jaw), who thinks Alim sold her a war crime; he sent A1B6 alone, then told her everything. A Hegemony "salvage assessment" team seized her manifest — they're *surveying* the rim. Recycler four is dead; a water riot is coming. **Deal struck**: Vora becomes the *face* of an Orizon rebellion under her own name (**Vora Kesh of Dust-Hallow**); her Berwick hauler (pilot **Osei**, clean papers, stripped registry, planted number-three coolant fault) carries Alim in as contractor **Dell Rask**, dermal tint over his optic (~4-day hold).
