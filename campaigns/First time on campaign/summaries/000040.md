**Alim – Profile**  
Human, mid‑20s rogue‑geek salvage runner. Circuit‑savvy, distrusts institutions, refuses to steal from the poor, keeps secrets, owns a battered utility rig and a self‑made astromech‑repair droid.

---

### New Play Events  
While held in a Level‑Ten holding cell, Alim explains that his “vault” is not a single silo but a decentralized blockchain. Every data core he stole is now replicated across thousands of nodes, making the dead‑man switch a global broadcast rather than a local alarm. The Hegemony official, who had planned to silence the transmission, is forced to reconsider. Alim negotiates: kill the broadcast in exchange for silence, but the dead‑man switch remains active. A pulsing camera in the corridor hints at a hidden observer—someone else in the vault is watching. Alim’s droid remains docked and ready; the Aurelian Spear drifts in the Outer Belt, a potential escape vector.

---

### Earlier Heist (Compressed)  
Alim and Kaelen breached the Hegemony orbital vault, spoofed the firewall AI, and stole encrypted data cores. A flare‑cap blinded sentinel spheres, and a bass‑heavy beat shattered their sonar. Alim initiated a dead‑man switch upload to his personal vault; the switch will broadcast the cores if Alim’s heartbeat spikes or the connection is severed. A massive armored security droid descended, capturing Alim and Kaelen. Alim was hauled into a holding cell, where a high‑ranking official offered to kill the transmission in exchange for silence.

---
