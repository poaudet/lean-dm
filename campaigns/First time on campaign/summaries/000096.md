**Alim – Profile**
Human, mid-20s rogue-geek salvage runner. Circuit-savvy, distrusts institutions, refuses to steal from the poor, keeps secrets. Owns a battered utility rig, a self-made astromech-repair droid, and a stolen Hegemony courier.

**Backstory (Compressed)**
Alim and Kaelen breached a Hegemony orbital vault and stole encrypted data cores. Alim wired a dead-man switch: the cores broadcast if his heartbeat spikes or the link severs. Both were captured. In a Level-Ten cell, Alim claimed his "vault" is a decentralized blockchain replicated across thousands of nodes. An official traded silence for killing the local broadcast—but the switch stays live. A pulsing corridor camera hinted at a hidden watcher.

**The Breakout (Compressed)**
Alim lured the watcher with a fake technical yarn, froze guards by shouting "We're not burglars—we are hobbitses!", and dropped electrified cables from a ceiling panel. A masked, distorted hologram—the Watcher—guided them out, an ally with unknown motives. Alim grabbed a black-market side-gig pad, ran a "ghost-mask" protocol to blur his signature into drone noise, and escaped. The Watcher gave coordinates for a blind-jump through a gravitational rift. Alim overclocked the drive and jumped—a violent transit that knocked out the hologram. They punched out near the battered freighter *Aurelian Spear*.

**The Sale (Compressed)**
Before docking at Orizon-4, Alim spoofed the courier's transponder as a decommissioned Hegemony luxury yacht—the *S.S. Celestia*. He landed at the lower docks of **Dust-Hallow**, a tiered scrap-metal port city, and pitched the ship to scavenger leader **Vora** (burly, clicking mechanical jaw). He floated the *Spear* as a second salvage and asked for parts, intel, and a data stash. Vora bought the "ghost-code in the BIOS" bluff: deal struck—50,000 untraceable credits, 48-hour access to her military-grade "Black Vault," and a threat to sell his organs if the BIOS claim is false.

**The Implants & The Raid**
In the Black Vault, Alim chose to replace his own eye—splicing an AR-overlay with a neural-link processor, bridged by courier ghost-code, for an embedded AI + HUD. Kaelen is horrified; Alim's set on it. The Watcher warned the ghost-mask didn't hide the ship—it created a sensor-void, a "hole" a High-Inquisitor's array reads as a neon sign. Three Hegemony Interceptors dove for Vora's warehouse to sanitize, not arrest. Alim flashed Vora a naive grin ("My bad!") and bolted with Kaelen into the "Sump," the lower slums, as the warehouse exploded.

**The Wait & The Return (New Play)**
The Watcher ordered them to stay put, blend, and watch: the Hegemony hunts something they left in the vault, and moves once the sector reads "clean." Alim observed that troops cluster on energy signatures—hunting tech, not people. As interceptors pulled back to the upper spires, the Watcher signaled the window was open, pointing to a derelict maintenance tunnel three blocks east. Alim and Kaelen slipped through the Sump, down the tunnel, and reached a shielded berth—the *Aurelian Spear*, intact. They boarded, reunited with the scolding astromech droid, and sealed the hatch. Alim ordered the droid to prep a surgical tray to install his implants.

**Where Things Stand**
- **Location**: Aboard the *Aurelian Spear*, docked in a shielded berth on Dust-Hallow's docking ring. Safe for now.
- **Data cores**: Secured in the cloud via dead-man switch; still live.
- **The Watcher**: Ally with unknown motives; warned of the sensor-void, is "coming for the cores," calls Alim "Hobbit."
- **Kaelen**: Aboard, rattled; trust fragile.
- **Gear**: AR/neural implants (uninstalled, about to install), high-clearance keycard, plasma-cutter, side-gig pad, 50k credits (digital wallet), droid + tools.
- **Threats**: Hegemony sanitizing the sector (hunting tech); Vora hunting Alim with a vendetta; *Celestia* likely destroyed; ghost-mask blown.
