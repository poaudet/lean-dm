**Alim – Profile**
Human, mid-20s rogue-geek salvage runner. Circuit-savvy, distrusts institutions, won't steal from the poor, keeps secrets. Owns a battered utility rig, a repair droid, and a stolen Hegemony courier (the *Aurelian Spear*).

**Backstory (Compressed)**
Alim and Kaelen breached a Hegemony vault and stole encrypted data cores wired to a dead-man switch (fires if his heartbeat spikes or the link severs). Captured, Alim claimed the "vault" was a blockchain across thousands of nodes; an official traded silence for killing the local broadcast—but the switch stays live.

**Breakout, Sale & Escape (Compressed)**
Guided out by the Watcher—a masked hologram of unknown motives—Alim ran a "ghost-mask" protocol and blind-jumped to the *Spear*. Spoofing it as a yacht (*S.S. Celestia*), he sold scavenger leader Vora (Dust-Hallow) a "ghost-code" bluff for 50,000 credits, Black Vault access, and an AR-eye implant. The ghost-mask made a sensor-void the Hegemony read; Interceptors torched Vora's warehouse. Fleeing, Alim tore off a boarding pod at 110% burn and—behind an iron-rich asteroid—used his neural-link, ghost-code, and blockchain nodes as a living supercomputer to crack the cores: names, funding for **Project Aegis**, and coordinates to the ***Silent Cradle***. Aegis syncs every rim relay to one source, ending all "off-grid" existence. Alim split the Aegis data into a thousand encrypted seeds across the blockchain, then—on the Watcher's invite—set course for a hidden nebula.

**The Signal**
En route, Alim's AR-eye caught a handshake from *inside* the decrypted data—from the Silent Cradle: a prime-number heartbeat, a distress beacon from an AI imprisoned in the Hegemony network. He quarantined it in an encrypted "voicemail" buffer, unanswered.

**New Play**
Waking in the nebula's fringes (hull 94%, fuel stable), Alim floated a "third party" seeking them without revealing the AI's nature. Kaelen wanted to fake their deaths; Alim insisted they're too deep to quit. Kaelen trawled the dark-nets and found a bounty on a physical-key forum: a reward for a "ghost-code signature" last seen leaving Orizon, posted six hours ago. The poster's handle is the *same prime-number sequence* as the voicemail. Worse, the post's encryption is live-issued Hegemony military—credentialed three years ago, before the heist existed.

Alim's theory: the "AI" isn't just tech. The signal modulated its carrier to a heartbeat; the vault firewall he spoofed matches the handshake signature exactly. Someone augmented and employed is wired into the Silent Cradle as the living cipher—screaming into a buffer.

**Where Things Stand**
- **Location**: *Spear* arriving at the rendezvous—five mismatched ships (freighter, two haulers, an ex-corvette). A suspicious woman hails, unencrypted: "Courier signature, you're late and you're not on the list. Identify." Voicemail pulsing faster.
- **Data cores**: Decrypted; Aegis intel seeded across the blockchain.
- **Silent Cradle "AI"**: Likely an augmented captive; beacon and bounty are the same actor working two angles. Voicemail still buffered, unanswered.
- **The Watcher**: Ally, unknown motives; calls Alim "Hobbit."
- **Kaelen**: Knows of a "third party"; unaware of the AI's true nature or full Aegis scope.
- **Gear**: AR/neural implants (active), high-clearance keycard, plasma-cutter, side-gig pad, 50k credits, droid + tools.
- **Threats**: Hegemony sanitizing the sector; Vora's vendetta (alive); *Celestia* likely destroyed; ghost-mask blown.
