**Alim – Profile**
Human, mid-20s rogue-geek salvage runner. Circuit-savvy, distrusts institutions, refuses to steal from the poor, keeps secrets. Owns a battered utility rig, a self-made astromech-repair droid, and now a stolen Hegemony courier.

**Backstory (Compressed)**
Alim and Kaelen breached a Hegemony orbital vault and stole encrypted data cores. Alim wired a dead-man switch: the cores broadcast if his heartbeat spikes or the link severs. Both were captured. In a Level-Ten cell, Alim revealed his "vault" is a decentralized blockchain replicated across thousands of nodes. An official traded silence for killing the local broadcast—but the switch stays live. A pulsing corridor camera hinted at a hidden watcher.

**The Breakout (Compressed)**
Alim lured the watcher with a fake technical yarn. Guards sealed him in an airlock kill-box; he shouted "We're not burglars—we are hobbitses!" to freeze them, then dropped electrified cables from a ceiling panel. A masked, distorted hologram—the Watcher—appeared and guided them out, an active ally with unknown motives. Alim doubled back to grab the guards' black-market side-gig pad (signal-booster, encrypted relay). At a maintenance junction above the hangar, he ran a "ghost-mask" protocol to blur his signature into maintenance-drone noise, picked an unmarked fast-attack courier, and shoved Kaelen down a disposal chute as a distraction.

**The Escape**
Alim slammed the guard's pad into the console, bypassing biometrics, and spun up the engines. He bellowed for Kaelen, who dove aboard as the hatch sealed. Punching through the atmospheric shield, they escaped into the void. The Watcher reappeared, warned that the Hegemony would lock every jump-gate, and offered coordinates for a blind-jump through a gravitational rift to the Outer Belt. Alim overclocked the drive and jumped—a violent, teeth-rattling transit that knocked out the hologram. They punched out near the battered freighter *Aurelian Spear*.

**Where Things Stand**
- **Location**: Outer Belt, near the *Aurelian Spear*. Orizon-4—a rust-colored frontier world with a black-market port—is close by. Plan: dump the courier, recover gear from the *Spear*, decode the cores.
- **Data cores**: Secured in the cloud via dead-man switch; still live.
- **The Watcher**: Just sent a message that survived the jump: "Don't get too comfortable on Orizon. I'm coming for the cores. Keep your eyes open, Hobbit." Motives unknown; ally status now uncertain.
- **Kaelen**: Aboard, rattled, wants a hospital and a drink; trust still fragile.
- **Gear**: High-clearance keycard, plasma-cutter, black-market side-gig pad, and a stolen Hegemony courier worth more than a colony.
- **Threats**: Hegemony lockdown on jump-gates; Sentinel AI may recalibrate; ghost-mask signature blur is fragile and time-limited.
