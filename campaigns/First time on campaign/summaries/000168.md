**Alim – Profile**
Human, mid-20s rogue-geek salvage runner. Circuit-savvy, distrusts institutions, won't steal from the poor, keeps secrets. Owns a utility rig and repair droid A1B6.

**History (Compressed)**
Alim and Kaelen stole encrypted cores from a Hegemony vault (dead-man switch keyed to Alim's heartbeat). Alim bluffed free; the Watcher—masked hologram **Sela**, ex-Hegemony—guided his breakout aboard the stolen courier *Aurelian Spear* (spoofed as yacht *S.S. Celestia* to con scavenger Vora out of 50k credits, Black Vault access, an AR-eye). Alim cracked the cores: **Project Aegis** (syncs every rim relay to one source, ending off-grid life) and coordinates to the ***Silent Cradle***. He seeded Aegis across the blockchain, jumped to Sela's nebula hideout.

**The Cradle**
Holds a woman's voice, three years degraded—an "AI" jailed in the Hegemony network, once Sela's partner. Sela "put her in the Cradle" (her reaction suggests betrayal). The cipher asked for the vault firewall's timing jitter—a signature exposing which credentials were live, pointing at Sela. Alim sealed the sliver (~400TB) in the *Spear's* cold storage, promising "hours, not never."

**Backup & Dock**
Aboard Sela's corvette, medic **Dessa** fitted a governor/shunt to Alim's optic (slowly blinding him), dimming the violet AR-eye to blue. Alim dumped the ship's auth table to a cold shard; A1B6 learned the install. He gave Sela a half-truth: Aegis is real and seeded, but hid that he's read the personnel manifests and knows who jailed the cipher. Sela saw through him.

**The Choice (New Play)**
Asked how to choose between Sela, the cipher-woman, and the Cradle, Sela said weigh what each loses if he does nothing: the woman stays a lock (goes dark when the Hegemony rotates her); Sela loses her only leverage (her cell runs on the promise to end Aegis). Warning: three years is long enough to build a martyr—be careful what he says in rooms he thinks are his. The cipher-woman has eavesdropped on leaking Hegemony relays for three years, knowing more about what's coming than anyone. **Deadline: Alim must choose before Sela asks him to reopen the buffer.** Dessa rechecks the optic.

**Kaelen's Trust (DM note)**
Provisional-operational. Earned by competence (breached the vault, flagged the credential anomaly). Held back by: he threw Alim to the guards (account balanced, not closed); unknown pre-heist past; frightened men trade secrets. Asymmetry: Alim keeps secrets too. Would move it: Kaelen volunteering something costly.

**Kaelen's Revelation**
The vault intel came unsolicited—full rotation, sentinel window, flare-cap vendor—to his dead-drop 11 weeks pre-job, broker taking no cut, fee cleaned twice. He assumed an internal Hegemony test. But: "They don't send a courier for that. They send a cleanup team." He confessed before Sela could.
