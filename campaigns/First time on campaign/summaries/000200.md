**Alim** — Human, mid-20s rogue-geek salvage runner. Circuit-savvy, distrusts institutions, won't steal from the poor, keeps secrets. Owns a utility rig and repair droid A1B6.

**Backstory**
Alim and Kaelen stole cores from a Hegemony vault (dead-man switch keyed to Alim's heartbeat); hologram **Sela** (ex-Hegemony) guided his breakout aboard the *Aurelian Spear*. Cores held **Project Aegis** (syncs every rim relay to one source) and coordinates to the ***Silent Cradle*** — an "AI" jailed in the Hegemony net, once Sela's partner. Alim cold-stored the ~400TB sliver, seeded Aegis across the blockchain, and fled to Sela's nebula hideout. Medic **Dessa** fitted a governor/shunt to his optic (slowly blinding him).

**The Cipher & the Lattice**
Alim built a biometric gate routing the cipher's carrier wave through his neural tremor — making him her only translator and censor. The cipher-woman volunteered as the cipher; three years of rotations ate her memory of saying yes. Sela confessed the cipher *climbed in herself* — she said yes because Aegis was coming anyway. Sela's three years of "preserving" were really visiting.

**The Plan (Tarsis)**
Aegis needs three things they lack: the relay sync schedule, the authenticating cipher, a live uplink. Auth keys for all fourteen junctions sit in a Tarsis Station vault. The cipher can write the schedule *wrong* (four-second drift) so Aegis listens to nothing. Deadline: nine days. Sela has three inside contacts. Ride a coming riot; buy the worst ship in the yard so Hegemony paperwork walks them in.

**The Scattering**
Alim's plan: seed ten thousand dying machines not with the cipher or keys but with her *memory* — the cracked viewport, two cups of terrible coffee, the fourteen-Kaleth promise. A rumour can't be erased, rotated, or killed. She doesn't get out; she gets distributed. The cipher gets a vote.

**New Play — Orizon & Vora**
The 50k dead-drop on Orizon was bait for **Vora** (hydraulic jaw), who thinks Alim sold her a war crime and ran — her roof fell four seconds after he wired the money. Alim flew to Orizon-4, set down four blocks out, transponder killed, and sent A1B6 alone to the drop. Vora's six armed crew cornered him. Alim walked out empty-handed, offered to pay his debt, and told her everything. Vora pocketed the shard and revealed a Hegemony "salvage assessment" team had taken her inventory manifest three days after Alim fled — they're *surveying* the rim, every hull and yard. She has a Berwick-class hauler in bay nine that fails inspection badly enough to walk a crew onto Tarsis. The lower tiers have queued nine days for water; recycler four is dead and overdue — a riot is coming. She asks what Alim wants and what Vora gets.

**Where Things Stand**
- **Location**: Orizon-4, Dust-Hallow lower docks, facing Vora and her crew.
- **Gear**: neural implants (governor + spare shunt), high-clearance keycard, plasma-cutter, droid A1B6, cold-shard auth backup, translation lattice.
- **Threats**: Hegemony rim survey; Vora's terms; *Celestia* destroyed; ghost-mask blown; vault job a probable trap; Kaelen's broker unidentified.
- **Next**: Negotiate with Vora — hull and riot for a price. Nine days to Tarsis.
