**Alim** — Human, mid-20s rogue-geek salvage runner. Circuit-savvy, distrusts institutions, won't steal from the poor, keeps secrets. Owns a utility rig and repair droid A1B6.

**Backstory**
Alim and Kaelen stole encrypted cores from a Hegemony vault (dead-man switch keyed to Alim's heartbeat). Masked hologram **Sela** (ex-Hegemony) guided his breakout aboard the courier *Aurelian Spear*. Cores held **Project Aegis** (syncs every rim relay to one source, ending off-grid life) and coordinates to the ***Silent Cradle*** — a woman's voice, three years degraded, an "AI" jailed in the Hegemony net, once Sela's partner. Alim sealed the ~400TB sliver in cold storage, seeded Aegis across the blockchain, and fled to Sela's nebula hideout. Medic **Dessa** fitted a governor/shunt to his optic (slowly blinding him). Alim copied the ship's auth table to a cold shard.

**The Cipher & the Lattice**
Alim built a biometric gate routing the cipher's carrier wave through his neural tremor — static to Sela's array, whole to Alim — making him her only translator and censor. The cipher-woman volunteered as the unextractable cipher; three years of rotations ate the memory of saying yes (she had to ask the date: Fourteen Kaleth, 4100 hrs). Sela confessed the cipher *climbed in herself* — she said yes because Aegis was coming either way and she'd rather be inside the thing that could break it. Sela only held the door; her three years of "preserving" were really visiting. The cipher needed her to keep coming back.

**The Plan (Tarsis)**
Aegis needs three things they lack: the relay sync schedule, the authenticating cipher, a live uplink. Auth keys for all fourteen junctions sit in a vault on **Tarsis Station**. The cipher can generate the schedule and write it *wrong* (four-second drift) so Aegis listens to nothing — until an audit finds the error in her. Deadline: nine days. Sela has three inside contacts (a supply clerk with watched credentials, a relay tech, one unnamed). Ride a coming riot (ration queues, dock fees, water-recycler failure); buy the worst ship in the yard so Hegemony paperwork walks them in.

**The New Plan — Scattering as Memory**
Alim's recommendation: stop keeping her alive, make her *unkillable*. Seed ten thousand dying machines not with the cipher or auth keys but with her *memory* — the cracked viewport, two cups of terrible coffee, the fourteen-Kaleth promise — the things that make her someone, not a weapon. A rumour can't be erased, rotated, audited, or threatened. She doesn't get out; she gets distributed. The cipher gets a vote. Kaelen: "the smartest thing you've ever said or the loneliest."

**The Dead-Drop**
Alim finally explains the 50k credits on **Orizon** (where **Vora**, hydraulic jaw, hunts his ghost): it's a landmine. Vora wired the money before the deal finished; her roof fell four seconds later. She thinks he sold her a war crime and ran. If he returns, she takes his eye; if he sends anyone, she takes theirs and learns he has a crew. Anyone who touches the drop is a fool or Hegemony — either way he learns something.

**Where Things Stand**

- **Gear**: neural implants (governor + spare shunt), high-clearance keycard, plasma-cutter, side-gig pad, 50k dead-drop credits, droid A1B6, cold-shard auth backup, translation lattice.

- **Next**: Nine days to Tarsis. Alim recommends the scattering; the crew assembles.
