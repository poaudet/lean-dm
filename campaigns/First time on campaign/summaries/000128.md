**Alim – Profile**
Human, mid-20s rogue-geek salvage runner. Circuit-savvy, distrusts institutions, refuses to steal from the poor, keeps secrets. Owns a battered utility rig, a self-made repair droid, and a stolen Hegemony courier (the *Aurelian Spear*).

**Backstory (Compressed)**
Alim and Kaelen breached a Hegemony orbital vault and stole encrypted data cores. Alim wired a dead-man switch: the cores broadcast if his heartbeat spikes or the link severs. Both were captured; in a Level-Ten cell, Alim claimed his "vault" was a decentralized blockchain across thousands of nodes. An official traded silence for killing the local broadcast—but the switch stays live.

**The Breakout & The Sale (Compressed)**
Alim froze guards with a nonsense shout, dropped electrified cables, and was guided out by the Watcher—a masked, distorted hologram with unknown motives. He ran a "ghost-mask" protocol, then blind-jumped through a gravitational rift to the *Spear*. Spoofing the courier as a decommissioned yacht (*S.S. Celestia*), he landed at Dust-Hallow and pitched it to scavenger leader Vora (clicking mechanical jaw), trading a "ghost-code in the BIOS" bluff for 50,000 untraceable credits and Black Vault access. In the vault he replaced his own eye with an AR/neural implant. The Watcher warned the ghost-mask created a sensor-void the Hegemony could read; three Interceptors dove to sanitize Vora's warehouse. Alim bolted with Kaelen into the Sump slums as it exploded.

**The Escape & Decryption (Compressed)**
They slipped through a maintenance tunnel and boarded the intact *Spear*. A boarding pod latched on mid-surgery; Alim's AR-eye came online and he ignited the engines at 110%, tearing the pod apart. Drifting behind an iron-rich asteroid, he used his neural-link, ghost-code, and blockchain nodes as a living supercomputer to crack the cores: names, secret funding for **Project Aegis**, and coordinates to the ***Silent Cradle***. Aegis synchronizes every rim relay to a single source—ending all "off the grid" existence. Alim refused leadership ("I hate meetings") but embraced being the glitch in the machine, fragmenting the Aegis data into a thousand encrypted seeds across the blockchain. The Watcher sent coordinates to a hidden nebula where an outcast fleet gathers: *"If you're the ghost, I can provide the haunts."*

**The Signal**
En route, Alim's AR-eye caught a handshake request from *inside* the decrypted data—originating from the Silent Cradle. He built a "digital airlock" (sandboxes, recursive firewalls, proxy nodes) and analyzed it safely: a prime-number heartbeat, a distress beacon from an AI imprisoned in the Hegemony network. He shunted it into an encrypted "digital voicemail" buffer, unanswered.

**New Play**
Alim and Kaelen slept, waking peacefully in the nebula's outer fringes (hull 94%, fuel stable, seeds replicating). Kaelen, still terrified but committed, trusts Alim over the Hegemony or Watcher. Alim floated the idea of a "third party" seeking them—without revealing the AI's full nature. Kaelen wants to ignore it and fake their deaths; Alim insists they're too deep to do nothing. Kaelen agreed to trawl the Outer Rim dark-net forums while Alim runs firewall cover.

**Where Things Stand**
- **Location**: Aboard the *Spear*, gliding into the outcast nebula.
- **Data cores**: Decrypted; Aegis intel seeded across the blockchain.
- **The Silent Cradle AI**: Trapped, beaconing; signal still buffered, unanswered.
- **The Watcher**: Ally, unknown motives; calls Alim "Hobbit."
- **Kaelen**: Aware of a "third party," not the AI or full Aegis scope; now researching dark-nets.
- **Gear**: AR/neural implants (active), high-clearance keycard, plasma-cutter, side-gig pad, 50k credits, droid + tools.
- **Threats**: Hegemony sanitizing the sector; Vora's vendetta; *Celestia* likely destroyed; ghost-mask blown.
